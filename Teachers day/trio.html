The Trio In My Thoughts :

On my way back from college in the bus yesterday, I spent the entire journey thinking that Jitendra Sir, Ashutosh Sir, Sujeet Sir—the three of you are the sole reasons I go to college every day. Who knows if any of you will teach me in the next semesters?

After thinking for a while, I felt as if something was about to slip away from me—something I desperately want to hold on to right now. Then I realized that the things I might lose are the three of you.

I also wondered if life will always be this way. Will some people enter our lives, become extraordinarily special, and then simply leave? Will there never be anyone with whom I can share a bond that lasts forever—someone who will never leave me?

Then I remembered that nothing in this world is permanent except God. And the Bhagavatam states that, "Everything we see is merely a manifestation of God Himself". So I thought that if I form a connection with the three of you while seeing you as forms of the Divine, this bond will remain permanent. Then Lord will reside in my thoughts through you, as well as physically present at college.

This Trio Is Deeply Special To Me. I don't need any friend, because I've got the three of you. Thank you so much for coming into this tiny life. I am immensely grateful to you and to my Lord, who gifted me this beautiful trio. Please forgive me if I have ever disappointed you.
